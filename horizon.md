The Forza Wiki is a community-driven encyclopedia about Forza: 
a racing game series for the Xbox,
-----------
Windows and PlayStation platforms,
encompassing open-world gameplay in the form of Horizon and track racing with Motorsport.

Forza Horizon 6 is the sixth game in the Forza Horizon series. 
It is set in a fictionalised version of Japan, 
where players take part in the Horizon Festival, 
beginning as a rookie driver and progressing toward Horizon Legend status.
